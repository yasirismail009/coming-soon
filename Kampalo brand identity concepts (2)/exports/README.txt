KAMPALO IDENTITY — ASSET PACK

Mark        single-path K: wide funnel mouth, narrow spout, one detached lead square
Descriptor  ASK. AUTOMATE. GROW.
Agent mark  Kai — purple squircle, prompt caret above an upturned arc
Type        Plus Jakarta Sans. Wordmark 700 at +0.10em tracking, all caps.
            Descriptor 600 at +0.14em tracking, all caps.

COLOUR
  Deep navy    #0B3049   mark, headings, buttons, gradient end
  Steel blue   #215F8C   gradient start, email header, accents
  Brand blue   #2563EB   UI accent, links
  Soft blue    #EAF1FE   chips, light fills
  Paper        #EDF0F6   mark on navy
  Ink          #0E1424   wordmark on light
  Purple       #6D4AFF   Kai on light backgrounds
  Purple lift  #8B6EFF   Kai on navy; lead square on navy

FOLDERS
  mark/       icon only — navy, paper, ink, steel, black, white, and
              purple-lead-square variants. PNG at 512 and 1024.
  tile/       contained icon — navy, gradient, paper, squircle. PNG at 512 and 1024.
  lockup/     horizontal (with and without descriptor), stacked, wordmark only,
              each for light and dark backgrounds, plus one-colour ink and white.
  kai/        Kai mark — light, dark, mono navy, mono black, bare glyph, and
              Kai + name lockups. PNG at 128, 512, 1024.
  favicon/    16, 32, 48, 180 (iOS), 192 (Android), 512 (PWA), 1000 (social avatar).

RULES
  Minimum icon size 16px. Clear space equal to half the mark's height.
  Descriptor is hidden below 150px lockup width — use the mark and wordmark alone.
  On navy, the lead square may sit in #8B6EFF; everything else stays one colour.
  Never recolour the K itself outside the palette, never add a gradient to the mark
  (the gradient belongs to email headers and buttons), never stretch the lockup.
  Lockup and Kai-lockup SVGs use live text in Plus Jakarta Sans — install the font
  or convert text to outlines before sending files outside your team.
